ABOUT KHWARIZMI CLUB

Learn beyond
the textbook.

Learn Beyond Limits. Speak Without Borders.

Khwarizmi Club is a student language community built around a simple belief: learning a language is more than memorising vocabulary. It is a way to connect ideas, understand different perspectives and express yourself with confidence.

Through reading, creative projects, practical challenges and thoughtful discussion, we make space for every student to grow.

I have keys, but no locks. I have space, but no room. You can enter, but you cannot go outside. What am I?

THE ANSWER: A KEYBOARD.
It has keys, a space bar and an Enter key — all language clues!

WORD SPOTLIGHT
Serendipity/ˌser.ənˈdɪp.ə.ti/ · noun

Finding something good or useful without looking for it.

Example: Finding a brilliant book by accident was pure serendipity.

LANGUAGE NOTE / 001

Lost in translation?

Some expressions carry cultural meaning that a word-for-word translation cannot capture. Context is part of language.

TRY THIS: What does “break the ice” really mean?
TRAVEL THROUGH WORDS / 002

Greetings matter.

Greetings can show respect, warmth, distance or familiarity. The right phrase depends on the person and the situation.

THINK ABOUT: How do greetings change between formal and informal settings?
